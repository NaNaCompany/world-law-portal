capital of the corporation was or became payable to, or
otherwise available for the personal benefit of, any
member or shareholder of the corporation; and
(b) all or substantially all of the gross revenue of the
corporation, other than dividends received from a tax ­
able Canadian corporation, in the year was derived
from an airport business.
